# Coraline Movie Summary

## Exposition
- Coraline Jones and her parents move from Pontiac, Michigan to the Pink Palace Apartments in Ashland, Oregon.
- She meets Wybie Lovat and a mysterious black cat while exploring the property.
- Wybie gives Coraline a rag doll that eerily resembles her.

## Rising Action
- Coraline discovers a small door in the living room that leads to a magical Other World.
- In this world, button-eyed versions of her parents offer her love, attention, and delicious food.
- Her neighbors warn her of danger, and the cat reveals the Other World’s sinister nature.

## Climax
- The Other Mother offers Coraline a permanent stay—if she agrees to sew buttons into her eyes.
- Coraline refuses and is imprisoned behind a mirror, where she meets ghost children trapped by the Beldam.
- She escapes with help from the Other Wybie and returns to the real world.

## Falling Action
- Coraline learns her parents have been kidnapped and returns to the Other World to rescue them.
- She strikes a deal with the Beldam: find her parents and the ghost children's eyes or stay forever.
- Using an adder stone, Coraline finds all three eyes and discovers her parents trapped in a snow globe.

## Resolution
- Coraline escapes, defeats the Beldam, and seals the portal with Wybie’s help.
- Her parents return safely, unaware of their ordeal.
- Coraline hosts a garden party and begins to tell Wybie’s grandmother the truth about her missing sister.
